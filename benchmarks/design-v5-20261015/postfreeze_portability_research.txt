Supplementary portability research, 15 October 2026 UTC

Timing and scope
These sources were reviewed after V5 source freeze and launch. They did not
change the running design, budgets, prompts, selection rule or protected cohort.
This is supplementary interpretation and future evaluation guidance, not a
retroactive rationale for the frozen intervention. No additional inference or
cross-model experiment was performed.

Yang et al., ACL 2025, Confidence v.s. Critique
https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.203/
https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.203.pdf
The study separates retention of initially correct answers from correction of
initially wrong ones, and finds different balances across model families.
Equation 5 expresses final accuracy as initial accuracy times retention plus
initial error rate times correction probability. Their prompt and training
results do not establish that our source-grounded critic will transfer.
Our inference: portability evaluation should report both transition directions
alongside overall accuracy, rather than treating more edits as improvement.
V5's post-terminal diagnostics already separate accepted edits, all critic-stage
effects including fatal auxiliary failures, and solver source-feedback edits.
Official first-attempt outcome totals remain authoritative.

Kamoi et al., TACL 2024, critical survey of self-correction
https://aclanthology.org/2024.tacl-1.78/
The review distinguishes prompted model feedback from reliable external feedback
and warns that evaluation conditions can overstate correction benefits.
Our inference: the executed probe and source checks provide useful external
observations, but do not supply a reliable oracle for natural-language intent.
Source-valid repairs can remain wrong. Host provenance checks alone therefore
cannot justify a generalization claim or an automatic semantic confidence score.

Fu et al., arXiv:2606.05670v1, controlled workflow comparison
https://arxiv.org/html/2606.05670v1
The authors compare normalized single and multi-agent workflows across ten
benchmarks with GPT-4.1; extra coordination generally fails to yield a consistent
advantage. Their separate external runtime study has different causal limits.
The paper explicitly describes one-run results and incomplete mechanism control;
its code release was prospective in the version reviewed. It is evidence for
careful matched comparisons, not a reproducible replacement for our controller
or proof that any particular context/verification mechanism causes improvement.
Our inference: keep model, tools, answer contract, accounting and logging aligned
and report total costs and failure paths as well as accuracy.

Transfer boundary
V5's question/program/probe/patch procedure has no question-ID, template-ID,
answer-key or model-name routing. The current FHIR workspace, citation checks,
completeness checks and answer interface are task adapters. Transfer to another
tool-supported QA task would require appropriate adapters and a new frozen
evaluation. A different model can change parsing, correction, retention and
resource behavior. The current run establishes neither other-model nor
other-task gains, even if its development score is favorable.
