Generic levers: prioritize question interpretation and temporal selection before
adding more solver budget or relaxing source checks. Any next critic should
inspect the actual executed computation and its question obligations, since a
textually anchored advisory plan and source citations can accompany wrong intent.

repeated unlinked metadata and long histories can consume prompt budget without
resolving source ownership. Preserve the fixed common cap and source requirements;
measure these mechanisms independently instead of treating rejected typed-correct
candidates as valid answers. These are design hypotheses, not per-question rules.
