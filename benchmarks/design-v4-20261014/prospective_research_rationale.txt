Why this next experiment
The completed V3d team scored232/300 versus fresh single238/300 (8 paired wins,
14 losses; -2pp, patient-block95%CI[-5.16,+1.09]pp). Nine paired losses were
source-valid wrong primaries: three calendar windows, three medication-reference/
dose mappings, and three concept/projection/microbiology interpretations. The
remaining five losses were three source-scope refusals and two provider failures.
All eight paired wins came from unmodified source-valid primaries.

V3d's283 static audits produced no material repair and zero targeted execution,
while consuming1.258M recorded tokens. Nineteen source-feedback revisions helped
four and harmed none; preserve that source/execution correction path. No paired
loss was locally token-limited. Therefore the principal hypothesis is that useful
semantic advice must reach the continuous solver before it commits to a query;
audit removal saves a phase without observed answer-level benefit. A larger local
solver allocation is a bundled secondary hypothesis, not a diagnosed cure.

Exact treatment to freeze
One question/context/shared-guide-only advisory planner:10k tokens, zero tools,
no answer/code/resources/selectedIDs/gold. Its bounded anchored JSON plan reaches
the solver from the first actual call; original question and guide remain
authoritative. The planner cannot filter tools, force fields/joins/empties or
veto a valid final. Invalid/UNKNOWN advice is ignored and recorded.
One continuous existing LedgerSolver:190k tokens,30 executed tools comprising
28 model-requested slots plus two counted source checks; at most20 real solver
calls and22 host slots. Preserve strict executed final/full-batch readiness,
existing one source-feedback correction, deferred scope/completeness/source
gates, same shared guide and fatal unknown-usage/overshoot policies. No late
static model audit, duplicate whole solve, vote or targeted semantic patch.
Aggregate stays200k/30. Same gpt-6-luna, current Azure/nativeSDK/observer/public
profile, timeout/retries/request cap, cache, fixed300 ordering and scorer.

This tests plan exposure, audit removal and local allocation together. It cannot
attribute a gain solely to planning or more roles. Advice can anchor a wrong
answer: one V3d blind contract itself gave wrong previous-year bounds. Existing
V3 source refusals may persist; do not weaken gates to recover gold-matching
primaries. Planner quote/schema acceptance, ignored advice, source-valid versus
null credit, source revision transitions and actual per-phase usage are required
outcomes. Paired losses informed this generic choice only; no per-question
guidance, template/gold inputs or runtime routes may be added.
