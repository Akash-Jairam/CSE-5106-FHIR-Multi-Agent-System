Append-only provenance corrections to the frozen design

The frozen design says full retrieved inputs are retained. V2 parse_outputs
actually retains retrieval IDs and full tool traces but drops the returned full
raw workspace maps. The after-run cache snapshot preserves 1,271 cache entries
(1,686,897,971 uncompressed bytes), may include unused entries, and does not
retroactively reconstruct every private role workspace. No pickle was loaded
when capturing it. The archive checksum and entry checksums are recorded in
cache_manifest.json; the local archive is ignored output, not a Git payload.

Medication physical request/page/cache metadata exists in the team trace, but
the single-agent dispatcher drops that optional metadata. Both use the same
retrieval helper; the audit logging is asymmetric. We do not claim symmetric
physical-request measurements for this run. The next operational storage change
will preserve full per-question trace and raw workspace before worker IPC,
using hash-checked gzip artifacts. That storage change is not an accuracy claim.
