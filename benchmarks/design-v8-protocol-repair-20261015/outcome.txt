Mechanism diagnosis
Among 23 nonfatal wrong-A cases, only 17 have released source-bound A artifacts;
six are primary-ineligible and one further case fails the outer residual check.
The 16 constructed snapshots per followup do not imply 16 actual model requests.
Actual auxiliary sends:W 12, R 11, C 0. Complete final B replies:W 6, R 3, C 0; all nine
are typed-wrong. No saved successful final-mode repair on these cases is correct
but hidden by witness/source release. R's three complete wrong-A Bs repeat A,
and it requests no new retrieval on wrong-A cases. All 11 R retrieval requests
occur on already-correct A cases. Permission alone did not cause useful evidence
acquisition here; this is not a causal claim that retrieval itself is harmful.

C makes no auxiliary send on these wrong-A cases because eligibility/residual
checks or the intact-context probe/final/judge reservation reject them. Overall
it sends on only 10/120. Its tie with A therefore does not show that continuation
reasoning was attempted successfully or that retained context is superior.

W exposes one real earliest-time tie but produces an incomplete, still-wrong
medication answer. Three of its six admitted wrong-A witnesses explicitly say
no counterexample was established. Source-linked text can therefore be retained
without demonstrating a fault. Separate candidate availability, fault discovery,
admission coverage, release and selection; a new selector cannot select a correct
alternative absent from the completed pool. See candidate_funnel/ for evidence.
