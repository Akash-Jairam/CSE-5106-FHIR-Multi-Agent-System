Hypothesis and intervention

V7 produced only one source-bound correct alternative to a wrong primary among
300 cases. A better selector alone therefore cannot produce the desired gain.
V8 changes candidate generation: an investigator sees the actual released
primary program and seeks a concrete violation of the question, observes an
executed probe or retrieval result, then computes a complete alternative. This
is draft-conditioned tool-grounded critique. The investigator and judge remain
fallible. Source validity and execution lineage do not establish semantic truth.

Run the existing capable Single naturally as A with its full 200k token, 30 tool,
30 SDK-send allowance. Do not reserve resources before A. Freeze its released
answer/error, executed program, source check, workspace, trace and model-facing
conversation in a content-bound envelope. A computed but withheld artifact is
ineligible for investigation. Every case remains in the denominator.

The first study forks the same actual A into three declared policies:
  W: isolated investigator, copied held workspace, no new retrieval.
  R: same investigator with existing patient-authorized retrieval available.
  C: same investigation procedure continuing Single's retained conversation,
     with R's retrieval permissions. Retained context is charged intact.
A is also materialized as its actual normalized result, without another solve.
An auxiliary output never enters a sibling's context, workspace or cache.
W's restriction applies to investigator retrieval tools. The unchanged original
source checker may still re-fetch cited records and return normal check feedback;
this is common to W/R/C and recorded in physical requests. W is not an entirely
offline policy or an absence-of-all-external-information control.

Investigation must identify a literal span in the original question/context
and retain an actual observation tied to executed code or successful retrieval.
There is no benchmark-ID routing, reference answer, SQL, oracle stop, query DSL,
exact-patch interface, trained selector or model change. The program is fully
recomputed and must satisfy the existing execution and original-source contract.
Fresh retrieval invalidates an earlier alternative computation.

On exact finite JSON answer agreement preserve A. On eligible disagreement use
one fresh tool-free judge over both complete candidates and the actual witness.
Switch only when its validated response identifies a concrete question-linked
contradiction of A and supports every declared requirement for B. Host checks
validate packet/source/execution bindings; they cannot certify interpretation.
Known auxiliary inability preserves A. Unknown admitted provider usage and
phase/global overshoot are fatal, including when A had been correct.

Budget and feasibility controls

Each policy counts A plus its own auxiliary work against 200k/30 tools/30 SDK
sends. Auxiliary work is additionally capped at 60k tokens/6 tools/6 SDK sends,
including judge, executions and source checks. At most two inspection/retrieval
interactions are allowed. No hidden retry or replacement of a failed first
attempt. The budget is an engineering screen, not a promise every path fits.

V8's B wrapper checks the actual successful final execution immediately using
Single's existing source validator. It does not require an additional model
prose-finalization send between execution and checking. This is an explicit
B-only procedural departure; A and the external Single stay unchanged. Budget
admission must reserve a complete judge packet and remaining final/check work.
Known refusal preserves A rather than adding a seventh send. The final freeze
must record the implemented path table, packet bounds and tests, including paths
where two probes leave insufficient room for a full feedback revision.
