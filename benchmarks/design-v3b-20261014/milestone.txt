Design

A question-only agent first derives a text-anchored query contract without
seeing the solver's answer, code, or patient records. One integrated solver
then retains its retrieval and computation context, produces an explicitly
executed final artifact, and checks evidence against the original sources.
A separate static audit agent compares the executed code with the contract
and retrieval ledger. It receives bounded samples rather than independently
recomputing the answer. Unsupported or uncertain audit findings preserve a
source-valid primary answer. At most one narrow host repair can change an
explicit numeric threshold or measurement ordinal, with replay, changed
contributor witnesses and source checks required before release.

The aggregate ceiling remains 200,000 tokens and 30 tools on the configured
Azure gpt-6-luna deployment. Contract/solver/audit token allocations are
10,000/175,000/15,000; tool allocations are 0/27/0 with three reserved for
targeted repair. Solver model calls remain capped at 20. Unknown provider
usage remains a fatal accounting failure. Raw traces and retrieval workspace
artifacts use lossless gzip storage. The SDK host-boundary conversion fix
passed actual-SDK mock tests and a small source-feedback transport check.
No shared single-agent inference or answer scorer was changed for V3b.
The full frozen design and research rationale are in design.txt/source.zip.
