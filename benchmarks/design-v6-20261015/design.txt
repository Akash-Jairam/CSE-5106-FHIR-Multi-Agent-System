V6 prospective design: continuous Single primary and independent recomputation
Prepared 15 October 2026 UTC. Experimental candidate; no V6 accuracy claim.
This prospective document is not itself a frozen substantial-run record.

Question and testable hypothesis

Can one small, answer/code-blind computation and a bounded disagreement selector
improve a capable continuous solver without sacrificing most of its solving
budget? V6 tests this on the existing adaptive fixed 300. It does not assume that
more agents, blindness, execution, agreement or correct citations imply truth.

The primary now composes the actual current SingleAgent policy rather than the
V3/V5 LedgerSolver. This and the auxiliary change are bundled: a win cannot be
attributed separately to natural continuation, removed Ledger scope vetoes,
independent generation, selector behavior or the allocation. Smaller primary
caps can still harm the actual Single policy relative to its 200k/30 control.

Why this experiment follows the negative evidence

V2's blind verifier used its own retrieval trajectory and could block release
when verification was incomplete; its team scored 198 against refreshed Single 231.
V3d scored 232 versus fresh Single 238; its valid static audit proposed no material
repair in 283 cases. V4's visible upfront plan scored 218 versus fresh Single 239.
V5's frozen-workspace executable critic scored 225 versus fresh Single 237, with
10 paired wins / 22 losses and reported team-minus-single CI [-8.06, -0.29] percentage
points. These completed comparisons do not establish causal component effects.

In V5, 282 primaries reached the critic, 334 replies and 97 tools were charged, and
zero replacements were accepted. There were 44 failed/unusable probes and 9 first
schema rejections; 52 second calls returned 27 UNKNOWN / 24 KEEP / 1 PATCH. The one patch
had no substantive executed effect. Of 64 source-valid typed-wrong primaries,
49 ended KEEP/UNKNOWN and 15 met mechanical/probe barriers. Parser repair alone
therefore does not address the dominant demonstrated gap. V6 removes V5's quote,
positive-source-fact, probe-observation and patch-witness interface; it gives B
one ordinary self-contained final-program tool call instead. One-shot execution
and selection remain difficult and can still fail or harm a correct A.

Corrected V5 transition interpretation, not a new score:
  Official available-draft -> released-final: 298 drafts, 224 initially correct,
  4 wrong->right and 5 right->wrong. The 23 generic revision flags are not critic
  repairs. On those 23 solver-source revisions, draft -> computed primary was
  5 helpful / 18 neutral / 0 harmful; draft -> released final was 4 helpful / 14 neutral /
  5 harmful. Six computed-correct primaries were subsequently withheld, and none
  reached the critic. Their typed credit is not proof that source gates should
  be relaxed. Both unavailable-draft rows received null/empty-reference credit,
  explaining 223 correct released answers among 298 plus 2 = 225 overall.

The source-scope audit distinguishes direct encounter/time membership from a
supporting reference and from search completeness. V6 makes NO shared checker
relaxation and adds no reference-path/time inheritance. Using actual Single
removes Ledger-only global unused-metadata vetoes as a policy difference; it
does not prove those metadata irrelevant or certify exhaustive retrieval.

Exact primary and eligibility boundary

run_continuous_primary creates actual SingleAgent(max_iterations=30), sets
175,000 aggregate prompt+completion tokens and 27 INCLUSIVE charged tools, and
lets SingleAgent.run(original_input) finish naturally. All native prompts,
starting packet, tools, tool-batch processing, compaction, post-final model
continuation, final automatic source check and one existing source revision
remain its normal policy. No Ledger subclass, first-artifact handoff, forced
explicit final from turn 1, budget nudge, 20-call cap or source-slot reservation.
Twenty-seven is NOT a guaranteed 25 requested + 2 source arrangement; automatic and
model-requested checks share that inclusive cap and may be refused naturally.

Adopt the primary's raw aggregate/per-call usage and actual tool count once into
a clean outer host. Retain its actual released answer/error/limitation and source
revision separately from its computed artifact. Do not promote a withheld draft.

B is eligible only when released A binds all of:
- a surviving actual successful mode=final tool execution and full five fields;
- the current held workspace digest and exact actual program;
- rendered answer/evidence/limitation equal to A and its final revision;
- the later actual final automatic_field_check_1 or_2 with exact citations,
  matching counts, status valid, empty warnings and issues, known usage;
- no subsequent cap-refused mutation request manufacturing stale eligibility.

Prose-only or otherwise ineligible primary output is preserved unchanged, with
an auxiliary-skip reason. It is not rewritten as a constant program, newly
source-checked, rescued by B or vetoed just because no executable A survived.
Fatal accounting is different: it withholds the overall attempt.

Auxiliary protocol and visible state

API: run_independent_recompute(host, primary5, artifact, workspace, context,
primary_source_check) -> (existing selected five-field candidate, diagnostics).
The context contains the original question, assumptions, patient and current
time. The actual original automatic source envelope is reused for A's eligibility
and summary; this costs no extra source call.

1. Freeze defensive snapshots of A, its actual code/artifact, held resources and
   context. Build B's fresh two-message input with the unchanged semantic guide,
   exact original context, workspace hash and held inventory. Inventory includes
   all held type counts/concept labels and one untruncated source example/type;
   these examples are previews, not a complete-record transcript. Oversized
   essential packets skip the auxiliary. B sees NO A code, answer, evidence,
   operation, conversation or source-check result. A's data can incidentally
   contain answer-bearing facts; it is not information-theoretic answer secrecy.

2. B receives only the existing native execute_python_code tool definition.
   It must request exactly one self-contained mode=final program, with no
   retrieval, inspect round, debugging conversation or second B attempt. The full
   held workspace is available at runtime, beyond the displayed examples.
   Intermediate examination and calculations may occur inside that one program;
   no intermediate tool feedback or second execution is available. Native
   argument parsing rejects duplicate keys/nonfinite JSON, unknown tools, extra
   calls/arguments or invalid final mode. The host runs the program once in a
   fresh OS worker receiving only a copy of frozen retrieved_resources.

3. Validate its ordinary AnalysisAgent five-field draft: answer, evidence,
   operation, contributing_ids, limitation. Require held full IDs, exact finite
   JSON, valid metadata and no limitation for eligible B. Use the existing actual
   check_fields once on B's exact citations and original question/patient/time.
   Require the actual raw envelope to bind every cited value/status/warning and
   matching counts. No synthetic clean envelope, Ledger unresolved-scope gate,
   implicit supporting-reference inheritance or alternative checker is added.
   Current ordinary established-empty rules remain: no preference for nonempty
   answers, and no empty answer may be inferred merely from tool failure.

4. Compare ONLY eligible A/B answers by exact finite JSON serialization. False/0,
   True/1,1/1.0,string/number,scalar/singleton and list order stay distinct. There
   is no gold scorer, timestamp normalization, semantic-equivalence oracle or
   answer-kind routing. Exact agreement preserves the COMPLETE original A and
   skips selection. Agreement is recorded as behavior, not correctness proof.

5. Disagreement admits one fresh tool-free selector conversation with original
   context, both intact programs, actual five-field artifacts, workspace/program
   hashes and summaries derived from actual clean source checks. Neither
   operation prose nor source validity is trusted as semantic truth. Exact output
   is {"choice":"A|B|UNKNOWN","reason":"brief concrete comparison"}. It cannot
   create an answer, change code/citations, probe, retrieve or call a third role.
   A/UNKNOWN, malformed or nonfatally unavailable output preserves A. A valid B
   choice releases the existing B artifact exactly. Selection can still be wrong.

Both candidates use A's frozen retrieved evidence. This is independent generation
conditional on shared retrieval, NOT independent retrieval or full source
completeness. No new SQL/fixture adapter, gold program, template/patient/question
ID route, model-specific branch, repair-class grammar or preference for B exists.

Exact resource admission and failure policy

                        Tokens incl prompts/output   Actual tools   Model calls
  actual Single primary            175,000            27 inclusive       <=30
  independent auxiliary             25,000            <=3               <=2
  whole team                       200,000            <=30              <=32
  contemporary actual Single       200,000            <=30              <=30

The implemented auxiliary uses TWO tools at most: one B final execution and one
original-record check. The remaining third slot is unused, not an implicit probe,
repair, replay, retry or free execution. Agreement uses one model call/two tools;
disagreement may use two calls/two tools. All attempted admitted calls are charged.
The possible32 team model attempts versus30 control is explicit; aggregate
token/tool ceilings match, model-call ceilings do not. Equal ceilings are not
proof of equal actual spending or cross-model computational cost.

Requested completion subcaps: generation4,000; selector1,800, inside unchanged
caller 16,384. Before B, count native tool schemas in the generation prompt and
reserve both calls, both completions and ordinary 5% / 64-token prompt margins.
The second reservation includes intact A/context plus an 8,000-byte maximum B
entry and 512-token serialization/framing allowance. B's code bound is 6,000
characters. A B entry consists of complete program, five-field artifact, source
summary and hashes; its structural byte bound is checked BEFORE the source call,
then against the actual checked entry. Recount the actual intact selector before
admission. No essential code, artifact or context is truncated to squeeze it in.

The 4,000 / 1,800 / 6,000 / 8,000 limits are the selected fixed engineering settings;
there is no accuracy-conditioned or per-question allocation. Initial three
reserved-development checks completed with 22/22 SDK successes. Two eligible A
artifacts led to B: one B executed with an explicit limitation and one failed
worker metadata with an empty operation. Both preserved A; the other A was
auxiliary-ineligible and was preserved. Both B programs failed to access the
held workspace. These checks exposed a generic packet-versus-worker visibility
misunderstanding, not an accuracy finding. The first old-prompt two-call
reservation 19,717 fit, but does not measure final-prompt population coverage.

Only GENERATION_PROMPT was then clarified: ALL HELD records are accessible at
runtime beyond displayed examples; intermediate schema examination/filtering/
ordering/calculation inside the one final program is allowed; there is no
intermediate feedback or second execution; held access is not complete retrieval;
operation must be a nonempty accurate string. Bounds, schemas, gates and call
counts are unchanged. The clarified reserved-development check then completed:
5/5 SDK successes; A used 4 model calls / 4 tools, B used 1 call / 2 tools. B produced
an actual valid mode=final artifact and original-source check issues[]. Its
entry was 4,172 bytes and the two-call reservation 19,910; exact typed answer
agreement preserved A. Full gzip restored, with no error or unknown usage. This
reused one previous reserved-development question, with zero protected 1397
overlap. No live selector path was exercised; that path has synthetic native
SDK/worker coverage only. One interface success is not an efficacy, population
coverage or causal improvement estimate. No labels/accuracy or alternate bounds
were used to select it. This candidate is ready for a prospective source freeze,
but this private packet is not yet frozen and V6 accuracy has not been tested.

Known pre-call refusal, B execution/source/schema/coverage failure, or verified-
usage auxiliary inability preserves A. Unknown/malformed admitted provider usage,
unreconciled adopted counters/cost, actual completion subcap overshoot or phase/
global token/tool overshoot is fatal for the whole attempt, even with valid A.
Original primary and attempted B remain in the audit; fatal output is not null
answer credit. Source/packet hashes and snapshot checks prevent stale authority.

Shared fault-path correction, required identically in both contemporaneous arms:
BaseAgent now labels post-provider missing usage/actual total overrun as Provider
accounting failure, so its ordinary known-preflight BudgetExceeded normalization
cannot turn those into unmarked null credit. The public Azure bridge preserves
native usage=None instead of LiteLLM's synthetic zero Usage. Explicit reported
zero remains known. Successful request options/defaultHTTPX2 transport, timeout,
reasoning, retry policy and valid-call Single behavior remain unchanged. Freeze
these shared bytes and the ignored runtime adapter in both arms; this is a
disclosed common infrastructure correction, not a team advantage.

Research rationale and counterevidence

CoVe (Dhuliawala et al., Findings ACL 2024) independently answers verification
questions to reduce copying/interference from the draft. This motivates hiding
A's reasoning while B generates, not trusting B or its later selection. CoVe's
factual QA experiments and multi-step verification are not a two-call FHIR/code
selection result. B is an open computation, not a draft-conditioned binary claim.
https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-acl.212/

CRITIC (Gou et al., ICLR 2024; first preprint 19 May 2023) motivates using actual
tool feedback and reports the importance of external feedback. Its official
program driver separately generates critique, rewrites and executes per iteration.
V6 does NOT have that feedback/repair loop or inherit its results. The copied
author code was pinned at ProphetNet commit 5cf70eb41cdaa1d8faa3e1265d95ee5792d49a53,
CRITIC/src/program/critic.py blob 3380050f5482cd2b2b1d5e49965c4b7fb1e95f61.
Gold comparisons there are evaluation/logging, not a permitted inference oracle.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.11738
https://github.com/microsoft/ProphetNet/blob/5cf70eb41cdaa1d8faa3e1265d95ee5792d49a53/CRITIC/src/program/critic.py

MBR-EXEC (Shi et al., EMNLP 2022) selects programs via execution-based agreement
among a generated set and approximates equivalence on test inputs. This supports
executing actual code rather than judging prose alone. Two disagreeing candidates
have no majority; V6's selector is not MBR or a correctness certificate.
https://aclanthology.org/2022.emnlp-main.231/

DPC (2026 preprint) supplies distinguishing SQL fixtures and an
independent paradigm. V6 deliberately does not implement that new task adapter
or an extra feedback loop. Its simpler fixed-workspace interface has less evidence
for semantic discrimination. Treat DPC as a contrast, not borrowed validation.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.15163
https://github.com/HKUSTDial/DPC/tree/f75c759e58cc5b2b2a0d9b5227e38536e9a248ad

The direct local counterevidence is V2's harmful mandatory verification, V3's
inactive narrow audit, V4's negative visible advice and V5's mostlyKEEP/UNKNOWN
critic, above. No source proves this same-model B/judge beats a capable Single
under 200k/30. A correct A can be switched to a wrong B; shared retrieval biases,
one-shot code/metadata failure and packet skips can erase any independence gain.

Generalization and later full evaluation

Do not inspect protected 1397 question-confirmation text/answers/programs while
refining V6. A development win only selects a candidate. The existing manifest
has 1,397 question-disjoint instances: 1,379 on 58 already exposed patients and 18 on
one nominally outcome-unexposed patient. It is not a patient/template holdout.
Once a candidate is selected, freeze one method/control and run the original
2,931 three-arm benchmark once; confirm team-minus-single on those 1,397 and report
all exposure strata and full 2,931. Require positive lower paired patient-block CI
for supported confirmatory improvement and team>single overall, as already
prespecified in evaluation_generalization_protocol_20261015.txt. No partial
confirmation scoring, repeated selection or promotion just because runs complete.
Failed/inconclusive confirmation is spent evidence; later confirmation requires
independent protected data. Repartitioning cannot recreate unseen patients.

The generic mechanism is isolated generation, immutable executable candidates,
original-source checks, bounded choice, fallback and explicit accounting. FHIR
schemas/IDs/clinical labels, patient/time scope and benchmark SQL time conventions
remain the current shared adapter. The implementation requires ordinary native
function calling and finite JSON, not hidden confidence or private reasoning.
Only gpt-6-luna is evaluated now. New-question, model, task, template and real-
patient portability remain separate unproven empirical claims. A later transfer
study must freeze orchestration and compare to each new model's capable Single,
with independent task/patient data and declared adapters rather than new case
routes or model-specific tuning. Cawley/Talbot and adaptive-holdout research
motivate the prespecified confirmation, not a guarantee of corrected overfitting.
https://jmlr.org/papers/v11/cawley10a.html
https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.02629
