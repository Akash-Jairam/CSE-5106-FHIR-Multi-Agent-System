TWO POST-V6 HYPOTHESES: RESEARCH ASSESSMENT, NOT A SELECTED V7
2026-10-15. Public primary-source review plus root-supplied aggregate V6 facts
only. No QA/results/config/patient data, Azure/model calls, source edits, or
implementation. The protected 1,397-question confirmation remains unused.

What V6 actually supports

V6 team 231 versus fresh Single 234 failed the prospective >239 AND >fresh
Single gate. Its recorded 175k/27 A-only outcome was 233/300. Among 220 blind
generation packets, only 37 B artifacts became eligible (12.3% of all300), with
29 exact agreements and eight disagreements. No eligible case had A wrong/B
right. Four B selections gave zero helpful, one harmful and three neutral
changes. One auxiliary unknown-usage failure lost A credit; four other team
fatal failures originated in the primary. These are the supplied completed-run
aggregates, not an inspection of individual cases.

Therefore allocation alone is not evidenced as the main cause. A-only233 versus
a separate fresh234 is also not a causal estimate of the 25k/3 reservation's
effect. Low eligibility and absent observed complementarity are separate
obstacles. Zero complementary correct B among37 does not imply zero probability
on other cases, another feedback policy, or another retrieval trajectory.

Hypothesis 1: full Single A, then own-feedback B on the frozen workspace

This would remove the deliberate reduction of A's allowance and let B react to
its own real Python execution/source feedback. It is a plausible repair of the
one-shot generation interface: an actual runtime/schema/source error is more
informative than asking the model to criticize itself without observations.
S* supplies mechanism evidence [R1], but uses public input/output tests. Our
interpreter and unchanged check_fields are weaker: they establish execution and
source-field support, not the intended operator, complete retrieval, or answer
correctness. A source-valid wrong query may receive no corrective signal.

This is the narrower engineering hypothesis. It can increase eligible B yield
without creating complementary correct answers. B remains blind to A's code,
answer and trajectory during generation, but shares A's held evidence and its
omissions. Independence of prompts is not statistical independence [R5]. More
iterations may repeat the same semantic error or consume the selector's budget.
There is no evidence here that feeding a successful result back automatically
improves it; stop or preserve A when no useful, affordable continuation exists.

Hypothesis 2: full Single A, then a fresh natural Single B with original retrieval

Start B from the original question/context, original guide and tools, empty
agent workspace/history, and the same natural Single policy. Do not expose A's
program, answer, selected resource types, retrieval queries or workspace. This
can change both information acquisition and reasoning, addressing a source of
correlation that hypothesis1 intentionally keeps. Read-only cache hits may serve
identical responses, but every tool invocation still counts; a cache does not
make queries statistically independent or provide new patient records.

Independent deep-search rollouts can increase candidate coverage, and tool-using
verification can exploit that coverage [R2]. Contrary evidence is equally direct:
general-agent rollouts improve oracle pass@K while actual self-choice often
fails to follow [R3]. A tiny remaining budget may not support the benefit seen
with many full rollouts. This is a residual-capped second Single trajectory, not
two full-cap Single solves. Keeping its natural policy means it can run out
before a valid final or source revision; do not force a convenient early answer.

This hypothesis more directly changes the evidence available to B. It is also
the larger treatment, with duplicate retrieval costs, fewer likely completed Bs,
and more complicated ownership of distinct source snapshots. Selection must
choose an existing source-bound A or B with its own workspace; merging evidence,
inventing an answer, or checking B against A's workspace would be another design.
Neither hypothesis has an empirical winning claim or selection authorization.

Exact residual constraints for either option

Let T_A, U_A and C_A be actual primary tokens, tools (automatic checks included),
and SDK attempts. After A naturally finishes under full200000/30/30 ceilings:
R_T=200000-T_A; R_U=30-U_A; R_C=30-C_A. No reservation, budget reminder, forced
final, early artifact handoff, source relaxation, or Ledger substitution may
change A. Under identical model responses this preserves its policy prefix;
separate stochastic runs need not reproduce the same released answer.

Reserve one intact A/B/UNKNOWN selector request including prompt, tool-definition
cost if present, framing, and bounded completion before admitting B. Then:
T_B+T_selector <= R_T; U_B+U_selector <= R_U; C_B+1 <= R_C.
An existing-candidate selector needs zero tools; verification probes would add
tools AND potentially SDK attempts and cannot be called free. Exact agreement
may save the selector call, but disagreement must have been affordable at entry.

For hypothesis1, one generation plus one execution plus one source check and a
disagreement selector needs at least2 SDK attempts and2 tools. A real execution
feedback revision plus selection needs at least3 SDK attempts and3 tools (two
executions, one final source check). Checking both B versions needs4 tools.
These are lower bounds, not promised sufficient budgets or a selected cap split.

For hypothesis2, with m natural B SDK attempts, r retrievals, e executions,
i inspections and s automatic checks: m+1<=R_C and r+e+i+s<=R_U, alongside the
token bound. Actual Single tools are inclusive; do not silently assume25+2 or
28+2 guaranteed source slots. Source-feedback continuation counts in m. Retain
its normal loop bound, while smaller residual resources may terminate it sooner.

Replayed B history, native schemas, tool results and all completions consume
tokens. An intact future selector packet needs deterministic size bounds or a
coverage refusal; essential A/B context cannot be truncated to make it fit.
Admit continuations only while finish reservations remain. Every admitted SDK
failure/retry consumes an attempt; unknown usage or actual overshoot remains
fatal, even with a valid A. Known pre-provider refusals are different. Preserve
the current provider/SDK/no-retry profile; do not copy research retry wrappers.

Freeze A's original released object, eligible artifact, source-check envelope
and workspace. B must run in separate state. Verified nonfatal B inability,
ineligibility, selector UNKNOWN or malformed selection preserves A. This protects
fallback integrity, not accuracy dominance: wrong B selection and accounting
failure can still lose a correct A. Full-cap A may leave no auxiliary resources;
report skips in the whole300 denominator. Resource-only admission implicitly
favors cheaper A trajectories, so it is not neutral coverage of hard cases.

How a future test could remain interpretable

Retain the actual full-cap A output for every first attempt before any B work.
Within that run, compare final team outcome to recorded A-only: source eligibility,
B complementary correctness, helpful/harmful selection, and auxiliary accounting
losses separately. This is a same-prefix stage counterfactual, not an estimate
of what another stochastic full-cap Single call would have answered. A fresh
paired Single arm is still needed for the primary comparison. Never select or
route cases using these correctness diagnostics; no extra model calls are needed
to record this A-only comparison. Keep null credit distinct from source-valid
release, preserve all errors/skips, and disclose actual resource distributions.

The first scientific hurdle is useful complementary eligible B supply, the next
is recovering it with fewer harmful selections than benefits. Improving only
eligibility or oracle union coverage does not establish team gains. Any future
candidate would need a prospective whole-cohort gate and the existing once-only
question-confirmation protocol; the adaptive300 cannot prove unseen-patient,
cross-task, or cross-model generalization. The papers below do not authorize a
new design, automated search, runtime score routing, or consuming protected data.

PRIMARY REFERENCES AND CODE AUDIT

[R1, reused] Li et al., S*: Test Time Scaling for Code Generation, Findings EMNLP
2025. https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-emnlp.865/
Mechanism inspected in https://arxiv.org/html/2502.14382v1 and author code:
https://github.com/NovaSky-AI/SkyThought/tree/0d190f11fd8e885bbe113aeccacba5ccde5b1102/skythought/test-time-scaling
SelfDebug retains its own code and actual public-test feedback and can stop once
public tests pass. Tests with correct expected outputs are stronger feedback
than execution success. Generated tests/judges remain fallible; oracle modes
are evaluation only. This code pin predates the November publication, so exact
final-paper/code parity is unproven; no shared200k/30/30 leftover result is shown.

[R2, new] Zeng et al., Pushing Test-Time Scaling Limits of Deep Search with
Asymmetric Verification, inspected arXiv v1,7October2025:
https://arxiv.org/html/2510.06135v1
The paper separately measures independent trajectory coverage and voting, then
adds a verifier that searches evidence for proposed answers. It counts searcher
plus verifier tools in its cost plots; it does not establish our token/tool/SDK
hard caps. Its reported GLM-4.5 average BrowseComp verification requires18 tools,
and larger configurations use16–32 solve trajectories plus verifier scaling.
The answer-verification asymmetry is task dependent: xbench has near-symmetric
cost, so that study omits verifier scaling there. Author code:
https://github.com/hkust-nlp/deepsearch-tts/tree/5b11ea97d280b9fde108095b56c13508eea852fd
solve_problem creates fresh system+question messages. verify_problem creates a
fresh question+predicted-answer conversation and actually searches. Its prompt
requests condition-backed confidence, not calibrated truth. deep_websearch can
invoke an auxiliary-model multi-step explorer, and wrappers retry; an outer
search count is not our count of every actual SDK attempt. Pin8Oct2025 predates
ICLR2026 publication; no final-paper/code parity or reproduced gains claimed.

[R3, new] Li et al., Benchmark Test-Time Scaling of General LLM Agents,
arXiv v1,22February2026: https://arxiv.org/html/2602.18998v1
Independent trajectories raise oracle pass@K, while pairwise/pointwise self-choice
shows a persistent gap and sometimes degrades; external GPT-5 pointwise judgment
does not reliably close it. Longer feedback histories can plateau or regress.
The study samples at most4 trajectories/model and reports estimated provider-token
cost; it is not our joint residual-cap experiment. Do not read its pointwise
conditional retention metric as end-to-end selected-answer accuracy. Author code:
https://github.com/cxcscmu/General-AgentBench/tree/35f5c027c31ddcb3366b28674c6cb2957460c0e2
Parallel script launches separate seed-specific passes. Pairwise runner makes
K-1 real tools=None judge calls and records usage; rankings select existing
trajectories. Minimal mode can compress/truncate trajectories, so these findings
are not exact evidence for our intact program/artifact selector. No full parity
or experiment reproduction attestation is made.

[R4, reused] BATS v2: https://arxiv.org/html/2511.17006v2
https://github.com/google-research/budget-aware-agent/tree/37d23f66c512734db476313cf436a506edbec9e0
Supports studying adaptive resource use; reports unified monetary token+tool

Section3.3/Algorithm1 andTable3caption describe100 per tool; surrounding Table3

[R5, reused] Kim et al., Correlated Errors in Large Language Models, ICML2025:
