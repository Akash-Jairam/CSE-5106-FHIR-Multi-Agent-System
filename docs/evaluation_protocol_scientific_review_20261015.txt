Two recommended clarifications before the eventual final freeze
1. The final analysis plan should specify a question-weighted paired estimand and exact bootstrap algorithm. Resample patient IDs retaining all paired questions and all planned repetitions; recompute total paired credit divided by total question instances. If three repetitions are planned, average the paired result across all predeclared repeats rather than pick a best repeat. A mean of patient means changes the estimand. Current documents correctly require freezing analysis/repetitions later, so this is not a blocker to development.
2. State explicitly that the patient-block interval is conditional on this cohort/task-template distribution and the assumed cluster sampling model. It accounts for within-patient dependence but not unknown cross-template dependence, prior adaptive selection, inaccessible pretraining exposure or external-site shift. “Question-disjoint” should continue to mean disjoint audited question IDs/outcomes, not guaranteed uninspected wording/templates or independent patient records. The manifest already carries this key limitation.
