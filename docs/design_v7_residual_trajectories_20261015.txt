V7 development specification: full primary, residual independent trajectory
15 October 2026 UTC. Candidate under implementation; no V7 benchmark result.

Hypothesis and evidence

Preserve the full natural SingleAgent solve, then use its actual unused resources
for a fresh SingleAgent trajectory with its own retrieval and execution feedback.
Compare existing answers against explicit requirements from the question. This
tests whether useful complementary candidates and selective replacement can
improve accuracy without reducing the primary allowance. The B policy, retrieval,
allocation and selector changes are a joint intervention, not an isolated ablation.

Completed V6 scored Team231 versus Single234 and failed its prospective gate.
Recorded actual A-only was233. B had only37 eligible artifacts and no observed
A-wrong/B-right candidate in that conditional pool. Most one-shot B programs
failed execution or their output contract before source checking. Fixing those
mechanics alone would not demonstrate semantic improvement. V7 reuses the actual
Single policy, including its documented output example and real feedback, instead
of adding a new special repair grammar. Independent retrieval can recover evidence
outside A's chosen workspace, but both runs still share the model, patient store,
starting-packet algorithm and possible cache responses. Independence here means
separate agent state and acquisition decisions, not statistical independence.

The fresh full-cap V6 Single control has299 known-usage outcomes and one unknown.
Known median residuals are137901 tokens,24 tools and24 SDK attempts;228 meet the
coarse joint100k/10/10 residual screen. These are different stochastic trajectories,
not V7 admission or completion predictions. Worst-case selector reservation is
substantial and must be included before B. Full A may leave no useful B capacity.

Research supports the hypothesis and also identifies its failure modes:
* ATTS studies completed independent trajectories and list-wise comparison, with
  stronger gains than routine reflection in its GAIA setting. Its released code
  has additional reward calls, a fixed judge and retries; it is not our inclusive
  residual-budget implementation: https://arxiv.org/html/2506.12928v1
* FineVerify compares candidates against shared question-derived requirements
  using independent evidence. V7's single tool-free comparison is a cheaper
  adaptation, not that method or a reproduction. No reference-aware preprocessing
  from its released grading driver enters inference:
  https://arxiv.org/html/2606.00660v1
* General AgentBench and AgentRewardBench show substantial verification failures.
  More candidates, valid citations and explicit labels do not guarantee selection
  accuracy or transfer: https://arxiv.org/html/2602.18998v1 and
  https://arxiv.org/html/2504.08942v1
Exact inspected repository/file pins and implementation caveats are retained in
the completed V6 post-run research record; no reported gains were reproduced.

Policy and accounting

A is an actual SingleAgent with its original prompt, guide, tools, starting packet,
natural30-iteration loop, execution feedback and automatic source checks. Limits
are200000 aggregate tokens and30 inclusive tools, reduced only when the caller
requests a lower global cap. Nothing reserves resources from A, forces an early
final, supplies a plan, or adds a release veto. Identical responses/tools should
produce the same A request trajectory and released output as the contemporary
Single control; separate live calls need not produce the same stochastic answer.

Freeze A's actual released answer/evidence/limitation/error, revision, trace,
workspace, usage and source-bound final artifact if present. Prose, limitations
and missing execution metadata do not invent a computed answer or prevent fallback.
Any nonfatal A can be considered for auxiliary work. Unknown primary accounting
or actual overshoot makes the whole attempt fatal and forbids B.

After A compute residual tokens, tools and SDK attempts from verified actual usage
under joint200000/30/30 maxima. Reserve one intact tool-free selector request and
its2000-token maximum completion before B. Entries are complete finite ASCII JSON,
at most32768 bytes each. Reservation includes the actual known A/system/original
question/context packet,32768 possible B bytes,512 boundary tokens, and the shared
5%/64-token prompt margin. This deliberately conservative bound can skip coverage.
Never truncate an essential program, evidence field or context to force admission.
The actual selector prompt must fit its reservation before its request is sent.
The shared32000-token input guard still applies; an intact packet that exceeds it
is refused without a request, rather than compacted or truncated.

B starts a fresh actual SingleAgent from the original question/context and the
same configured domain/model adapter and perturbation. It sees no A answer,
program, history, workspace, selected types or queries. Its original starting
packet is initialized internally by the unchanged Single policy. The additional
B initialization is explicitly charged as one host tool within the global30;
the common A/control initialization stays unchanged and is excluded equally.
This is additional I/O, even on a cache hit. There is no duplicate host prefetch.

B's token cap is residual tokens minus the selector reservation; its inclusive
natural tool cap is residual tools minus the initialization charge; its model
iteration/attempt allowance is at most residual SDK attempts minus one selector.
Admission requires at least two B model attempts and two B tools after those
reservations. After the counted internal initialization, B's ordinary pre-provider
check rechecks the actual initial packet/native-schema prompt. It may refuse that
request with no SDK send; initialization remains charged. There is no
forced early final or guaranteed finishing capacity: B can exhaust its allowance
before producing an eligible artifact. Automatic checks, feedback, all actual
requests, replayed prompts and executed tools count. Every actual SDK attempt is
bounded before sending, in addition to the natural iteration limit. No retries,
hidden synthesis, uncounted auxiliary requests or correctness-based routing.

Source, selection and fallback

B can replace A only through its actual released final artifact, actual execution
on its own unchanged workspace and actual clean original-source check. A computed
candidate later withheld by Single is never substituted for the released result.
Honest limitations remain visible; deleting them cannot create eligibility.
Original source checking and the common typed scorer remain unchanged.

Exact finite JSON answer agreement preserves the whole actual A. On disagreement,
one fresh tool-free selector receives the original task/context and intact entries:
actual released outcomes, eligible programs/artifacts, source summaries, workspace
identities and actual successful retrieval-request metadata. Missing provenance is
labelled missing. Single retains request arguments and a saved-variable/count
reply, while physical pagination/cache metadata may be unavailable; those retained
observations are not evidence of exhaustive retrieval. The selector receives the
unchanged shared FHIR semantic guide and compares both against question-derived
requirements with supported/not_found/contradicted judgments and concrete bases.
It returns an existing-candidate A/B/UNKNOWN choice, not a third answer or repair.
The host accepts B only when all listed B requirements are marked supported and
at least one shared A requirement contradicted. Missing A provenance, a limitation
or a not_found label alone cannot justify replacement. These model labels can be
wrong or incomplete; the acceptance rule does not certify correctness or coverage.

Malformed, inconclusive, oversized or unaffordable auxiliary output, unfinished B
and known pre-provider refusals preserve the actual A/error state. Unknown admitted
usage, malformed accounting or actual completion/phase/global overshoot is fatal,
even after a correct A. Auxiliary local budget markers must not leak into outer
formatting and replace A with a synthetic null. This policy protects fallback
identity, not an accuracy floor: a false B selection or later fatal request can
still lose primary credit.

Keep two versioned candidate snapshots under the existing lossless owned audit
mechanism. Each retains its own workspace, released result/error, actual program,
source result, usage and binding. Tagged phase traces preserve local tool-call
identities without confusing repeated automatic-check IDs. The final displayed
workspace belongs to the selected candidate; no silent A/B resource union. Large
unchosen snapshots must externalize before worker IPC and row accumulation.

Validation and evaluation

Before any V7 large run: independent offline tests of actual Single policy-prefix
equality, source/withholding semantics, state isolation, phase adoption exactly
once, per-call and aggregate accounting, native SDK boundary, candidate reservation,
strict selection, fatal/nonfatal fallback and owned gzip restoration. Synthetic
fixtures vary values, order, missing versus empty data and failed operations;
these are engineering checks, not accuracy experiments. Any live interface check
uses only the pre-reserved development pool, never protected confirmation questions.

Freeze exact tested implementation, source/runtime archives, inputs, request profile,
commands, analysis and integrity guard before fresh300 Single+300 Team first attempts.
No partial accuracy, retries, selective exclusions or best-attempt replacement.
Selection requires Team>239 AND Team>the fresh Single control. Record whole-cohort
accuracy, paired patient-block uncertainty, errors, source/answer eligibility,
B complementary correctness, selection help/harm, A-only stage counterfactual,
resource distributions and all admission skips. A-only is the actual same-prefix
outcome, not another stochastic control or a causal allocation ablation.

The adaptive300 is development only. A selected candidate may use the prespecified
once-only full2931-by-three-arm run and1397 question-disjoint confirmation, with no
partial full scores until every attempt is terminal and audited. This is not a
patient-disjoint test. Do not change the protected protocol after results. No
cross-model or other-task tests are required now; portability remains an unmeasured
goal. No benchmark ID, patient/template ID, reference SQL, answer key, cached
correctness or model identity may control inference. FHIR interpretation stays in
the common domain adapter. Main changes only after a completed acceptance gate.
