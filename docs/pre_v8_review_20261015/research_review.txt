Pre-V8 research review — 15 October 2026

This review supports a proposal, not a claim that a new design works. No model
inference, patient-store requests, training, candidate-program replay or V8
implementation was performed. Public author code was inspected as text. Source
versions and seven newly inspected author files are in research_sources.json;
the existing complementary-verification audit also inspected 17 author files.
No public benchmark answer dumps or newly protected project cases were opened.

Research conclusion

The strongest reason to add another agent is an observable difference in its
information-producing task. Another answer, a valid citation, successful code,
an agreeing critic, or a larger checklist does not demonstrate that difference.
The proposed investigation must expose a concrete violation of the question by
the actual program, then produce a complete, executed alternative. Whether this
beats a capable single agent remains an empirical question. More elaborate
topologies, a new framework, or a new query language are not justified merely
by the previous variants' failure.

Directly relevant EHR evidence

FHIR-AgentBench separates retrieval from interpretation failures. Its analysis
includes wrong resource types, narrow searches, unresolved medication links,
incorrect encounter filtering and wrong concept fields. Its original agent
already retrieves into an out-of-context Python workspace and iterates on code.
We cannot sell that existing capability as a new multi-agent mechanism. The
published full-benchmark, different-model, LLM-judge results are not numerical
baselines for our adapted subset and typed scorer. Our current Single also
adds execution-bound answers, shared semantics and original-source checks.
https://arxiv.org/html/2509.19319v2
https://proceedings.mlr.press/v297/lee26a.html
Author code inspected at bbb42909a5a7eb907d1cd91f72a560729e7037ea:
https://github.com/glee4810/FHIR-AgentBench/blob/bbb42909a5a7eb907d1cd91f72a560729e7037ea/agent/multi_turn_code_resource_agent.py

FHIRPath-QA makes query execution explicit, but does not eliminate semantic
errors. Its analysis includes wrong resource properties, prescription versus
administration confusion, distinct-event mistakes and relative-date errors.
Fine-tuning transfers better to some new compositions/paraphrases, but worsens
performance on held-out resource types. Its entire cohort is the same MIMIC
FHIR demo. Its query-first setup can evaluate over a complete patient bundle;
that is a different acquisition condition from our bounded live-tool agent.
Its split script assigns paraphrase/template holdouts, not a new patient cohort.
Thus a FHIRPath rewrite is a separate representation/tool hypothesis requiring
a Single control with the same executor, not an established coordination fix.
https://arxiv.org/html/2602.23479v1
https://github.com/mooshifrew/fhirpath-qa/blob/3b995f15aac7c56c8e98ea23e87eb34195727ca8/add_splits_to_dataset.py

EHRNavigator demonstrates heterogeneous structured/note retrieval and multiple
backbones. However, its reported comparisons include vanilla full-schema or
raw-context prompting, pretrained text-to-SQL models and RAG. They do not isolate
our question: collaboration versus a natural iterative Single with identical
tools and aggregate caps. Its manuscript says code will follow acceptance.
It motivates modular evidence acquisition; its headline gains do not predict a
win over our stronger control.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.10020

EHRAgent's relevant contribution is interactive executable reasoning with domain
knowledge, already represented in our Single's capabilities. Its published
abstract reports multi-dataset benefits, but those are not evidence that an
additional verifier improves this already executable baseline. We do not claim
to have reproduced it or newly audited its complete implementation in this phase.
https://aclanthology.org/2024.emnlp-main.1245/

Verification mechanisms and their limits

CRITIC obtains external search/interpreter observations before revision. Its
ordinary correction results include harm; oracle variants use correctness
knowledge unavailable at deployment. The inspected author implementation has
real multi-step interactions and retries, so round counts are not actual-send
counts. Our V5's frozen workspace, one inspection and exact patch interface
were materially narrower. The transferable mechanism is evidence-conditioned
investigation, not the paper's effect size or oracle stopping. It supports
testing full-program repair after a concrete observation, while measuring
right-to-wrong changes and charging all interactions.
https://arxiv.org/html/2305.11738v4
https://github.com/microsoft/ProphetNet/tree/5cf70eb41cdaa1d8faa3e1265d95ee5792d49a53/CRITIC

Chain-of-Verification separates verification answers from the draft and finds
open questions useful. Its controlled main setting is closed-book verification,
without external tools; a separately prompted answer remains a model belief.
It motivates an anchoring ablation, not treating a question-only contract as
truth. A fully factored planner/checker/repair chain would add stages and must
first demonstrate affordability within actual residual resources. We should
not add it to the initial intervention as an unmeasured bundle.
https://arxiv.org/html/2309.11495v2
https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-acl.212/

RARR finds that neutral information requests can be more useful than searches
that echo an existing claim. It gates edits on evidence disagreement. Better
attribution nevertheless does not imply better downstream reasoning: QA results
are mixed. The author implementation additionally uses retrieval/ranking and
repeated model calls. For us, a retrieved source must answer an open information
need, and a local correction must survive recomputation of the whole answer.
Neither an attribution score nor minimal textual edits substitute for accuracy.
https://arxiv.org/html/2210.08726v3
https://github.com/anthonywchen/RARR/tree/51a1a10fe5bada837a368f98cb55288ac5168c9e

What structured-query research does and does not justify

CHESS uses distinct retrieval, schema, generation and testing responsibilities.
Its high-budget configuration generates 20 candidates and 10 tests; its lower
budget configuration omits the tester. Crucially, author code generates natural
language tests from candidate queries and execution clusters, then asks an LLM
to score them. Those are fallible semantic judgments, not executable unit-test
proofs. Some model configurations mix fine-tuned and proprietary models; the
correct-context analysis uses gold-aligned schema as an upper-bound diagnostic.
We can borrow the idea of exposing semantic differences, while declining its
compute, training and oracle assumptions. Narrowing schema can also remove
needed information and should not be automatically added here.
https://arxiv.org/html/2405.16755v3
https://github.com/ShayanTalaei/CHESS/blob/3d6e835f858d26885d21d4bc0215aeecf855efbe/src/workflow/agents/unit_tester/tool_kit/evaluate.py
https://github.com/ShayanTalaei/CHESS/blob/3d6e835f858d26885d21d4bc0215aeecf855efbe/src/workflow/agents/unit_tester/tool_kit/generate_unit_test.py

CHASE-SQL deliberately changes generation strategies, rather than relying only
on repeated identical prompts. Its selected accuracy also depends on a trained
pairwise selector. Its budget and supervision differ from ours. This supports
measuring useful candidate complementarity separately from selection, not
copying its headline performance into a fixed-model, residual-budget proposal.
Third-party repositories found in search were not treated as author code.
https://arxiv.org/html/2410.01943v1

SQL-of-Thought is an important exclusion. Section 3.1 explicitly describes
comparing execution with ground truth to trigger and repeat correction. Other
descriptions emphasize execution failures, leaving a consequential protocol
ambiguity. Without evidence of a gold-free deployed loop, its headline score
cannot justify our verifier. A useful error taxonomy does not authorize access
to reference answers or a correctness-triggered router.
https://arxiv.org/html/2509.00581v2

Distilled SQL test suites show why agreement on one database can hide different
query meanings. Their tests are constructed around gold queries for evaluation.
They cannot be used as a gold-free inference oracle. The applicable development
lesson is to generate independent specification fixtures with ties, boundaries,
irrelevant rows and different projections; a candidate that passes those tests
still needs empirical QA evaluation on new data.
https://arxiv.org/html/2010.02840v1
https://aclanthology.org/2020.emnlp-main.29/

Architecture, cost and transfer

The agent-scaling study finds task- and architecture-dependent outcomes under
controlled resources. Strong Single performance and coordination overhead are
relevant to experiment design; a reported capability threshold is not a universal
law for our model or dataset. We need a same-context followup control before
attributing an improvement to an isolated agent.
https://arxiv.org/html/2512.08296v3
https://www.nature.com/articles/s42256-026-01268-y

Correlated Errors measures shared errors among models, not our conditional
trajectory distribution. Its implication here is a research hypothesis: nominal
agent diversity does not ensure useful error diversity. Our direct V6/V7 joint
outcomes supply the stronger local evidence. We should count correct alternatives
to wrong A outcomes, rather than treating disagreement rates as useful diversity.
https://arxiv.org/html/2506.07962v1

MAST distinguishes system specification, interaction and verification failures.
It supports tracing where a proposed correction failed rather than calling every
wrong final answer a reasoning failure. We use that distinction qualitatively;
no external judge labels or paper-level category proportions are imported into
our local cases.
https://openreview.net/pdf?id=fAjbYBmonr
https://sites.google.com/berkeley.edu/mast/home

The recent FHIR RL work changes learned model behavior through supervised reward
signals and repeated environment interaction. It is relevant evidence that the
retrieval/interpretation policy is learnable, but training and a changed backbone
are separate research questions from fixed-Luna orchestration. We do not use its
score as a directly comparable control or propose training in this phase.
https://arxiv.org/html/2605.14126v1

An exploratory FHIR MCP repository documents controls for tool-catalog, context
and code-execution effects, including incomplete historical reproducibility.
It is a useful engineering lead, not peer-reviewed causal evidence or a numerical
replication performed here. Only its README and roadmap were consulted; answer
dumps were deliberately avoided because they reuse our benchmark.
https://github.com/coralehr/fhir-mcp-eval

Standards versus adapter conventions

FHIR R4 distinguishes an observation's effective time from when that version was
issued, and permits absent values and linked specimen/context information. These
are schema semantics. Our shifted wall-clock policy, identifier conventions,
relative-date rules and what constitutes the benchmark's requested admission
label are adapter/benchmark conventions, not universal FHIR rules. Any correction
to those shared conventions must be offered to Single too. Standards do not prove
that one fetched type contains every event relevant to a natural-language question.
https://hl7.org/fhir/R4/observation-definitions.html
https://hl7.org/fhir/R4/search.html

Decision

Prioritize a bounded counterexample investigation with actual probes, full
recomputation, unchanged source checks and explicit fallback. First test whether
it produces useful observations and corrective alternatives; then test whether
role isolation or additional retrieval contributes. Defer a general query DSL,
trained selector, higher-cap run and elaborate factored verifier until a specific
failure and a controlled comparison justify them. Research supplies mechanisms
and failure predictions; it does not supply evidence that the proposed system
already improves accuracy or generalizes.
