2. Why another blind rollout is weakly motivated

Fresh state removes direct copying but retains the same model policy, guide,
starting-packet algorithm, source store and often cached observations. Errors
can share unobserved question difficulty and the same wrong interpretation;
independently issued requests do not make their errors statistically independent.
Cross-model correlated-error evidence reinforces the warning, but is not a
measured correlation coefficient for our FHIR trajectories [R2].

Resources also select coverage. V7 entered B268/300, but81/268 known B budget
stops (~30.2%) occurred, versus13 A stops. Worst-case selector reservation and
B initialization consume resources before useful investigation. This is evidence
against treating coarse residual-token availability as completed-solve capacity;
it does not isolate budget starvation as the cause of failed complementarity.
The reported mean-token ratio Team/Single is1.593. These partial recorded means
exclude unknown failed usage and are not complete billing or FLOPs. Equal
200k/30/30 maxima are a legitimate operational comparison; they do not imply
equal actual expenditure or efficient use of additional expenditure.

3. Critique of the proposed targeted investigator

A copied investigator that receives A's program plus a literal question obligation
and can seek new source records is a reasonable, distinct hypothesis. Ask it to
expose a concrete omitted/contradicting operand or computation, then generate a
complete alternative program and release only its real executed/source-bound
result. Keep inconclusive fallback and current unknown-usage/fatal policy.

Do not call a model-written spec an oracle. An executable wrong time window,
cohort predicate or aggregation is still wrong. A universal DSL adds translation,
coverage and compiler failures and risks making FHIR assumptions appear universal.
A concise untrusted investigation plan/probe is enough for the first test. A
separate question-only obligations stage is worth a later isolated ablation only
if the basic investigator shows useful opportunity and evidence of draft-induced
anchoring remains. It need not be a mandatory initial stage. Host-checking that a
quoted span occurs literally in the question checks quotation, not interpretation.
If an
executable specification is later necessary, test its faithfulness independently
against handwritten specification fixtures and ambiguous/missing cases; do not
only test parser validity or agreement with A. Any new executor/domain semantics
must also be available to the Single control.

Persistent held-record errors in root's independent audit motivate testing derived
observations as well as new retrieval: wrong field choice, missing tied extrema
and chronology operands can survive a fresh full solve with all facts present.
Those are error classes to challenge with independent fixtures, not per-case
repair rules. Ambiguous source/reference mappings remain unresolved/adjudicated.

New records are not automatically new information, and a retrieved counterexample
is only relevant if its question-derived predicate is correct. Measure whether
investigation actually changes a required operand, scope or result; include false
counterexamples on correct A. Empty retrieval, source-field success and LLM labels
cannot certify search completeness or semantic contradiction. FineVerify motivates
shared explicit requirements plus fresh evidence, but does not certify our
lighter adaptation [R3]. Root's separate code audits identify evaluation-boundary
and cost caveats; they were not independently rerun here.

Preserve full natural A. Any separate question-only planning/spec call must occur
after A in a fresh blind context and use residual resources; running it first
quietly reduces A unless the experiment explicitly changes. Prefer the plan as
an investigator action over another mandatory LLM stage. Count investigation,
retrieval initialization, replayed prompts, complete alternative/source check,
selection and failed requests. Show joint residual admission and actual finishing
capacity on fixed development before another large run; do not reuse V7's old
admission counts for a longer protocol.

4. Minimal empirical evidence before another300

First use exposed failures to classify, rather than patch, the main loss mechanisms:
wrong obligation, missing acquisition, incorrect computation, source withholding,
resource exhaustion, and provider failure. Include correct-A controls. This
human/agent development analysis is not a new held-out test.

Then preregister ONE bounded mechanism study on excluded development/pilot
patients, with a metadata-chosen fixed batch across families/patients. A possible
budget is120 questions across at least20 available development patients; this is
an engineering screen, not a powered accuracy claim. Record substantial model
comparisons and every first outcome. No run is proposed while the goal is paused.

Primary contrast: the SAME investigator/inputs/allowance with fresh retrieval
versus immutable A-workspace-only investigation. This isolates information access
within that scaffold. Both arms can generate new executable DERIVATIONS from held
records; workspace-only success still supports the investigator hypothesis, and
retrieval-on need not win to make the procedure useful. Do not change prompts/selector/budgets simultaneously and
call it an information-access ablation. If opportunities emerge, a second narrow
contrast compares the investigation procedure as same-context Single self-check
versus an isolated role; this tests whether procedure/new evidence or role
separation is responsible. A prior historical V7 run is background, not a matched
current ablation. A-only remains the immediate preservation/control comparison.
Keep a fresh capable Single in the eventual whole-system comparison.

For each fixed batch report O=#wrong-A/correct source-bound alternative, actual
helpful/harmful switches H/M, correct-A auxiliary fatal loss F, and net H-M-F,
all over the entire batch. Also report all actual releases, computed-withheld
finals separately, eligibility/admission, false counterevidence, resource use,
and which initially wrong question obligations changed. Raw disagreement or a
successful program is not a go signal.

A positive opportunity gate should be a predeclared engineering decision, not an
invented significance claim. Choose a minimum worthwhile point gain delta_min
and an allowance L for harm/failure, then require O >= ceil(N*delta_min)+L and
positive observed net effect before spending another300. For illustration ONLY,
N120, delta_min3pp and L2 gives O>=6, with opportunities spanning multiple patients
and at least two task structures. Those particular numbers are a proposed cost/
feasibility screen, not estimated power or universal thresholds. If opportunity
remains ~1/300, changing the selector cannot support the goal. If results miss
this screen, record the negative mechanism study rather than quietly tune its
threshold. Report uncertainty even if the screen passes.

Independent synthetic/metamorphic specifications should vary event order, units,
linked dependencies, empty versus missing data, irrelevant records, temporal
boundaries and failed tools. Add negative controls where A is correct. A frozen
batch of newly authored compositions or generator seeds should remain unseen
until the test. These establish engineering behavior, not clinical/task transfer.

5. Budget fairness and uncertainty

Keep the current max200k/30tools/30SDK, first-attempt, source validation and
unknown-usage rules unless a NEW experiment prospectively changes them. Same
maxima answer whether the policy helps under that ceiling. To claim efficiency,
also compare accuracy-resource frontiers with an equally capable Single
verification/continuation control, across a small EX-ANTE budget grid. Do not
force Single to waste tokens to match a realized bill, post-select cases with
similar observed costs, or discard failures with unavailable usage. Actual
spending depends on difficulty/stopping/failure; matching it after outcomes can
bias comparison. Domain/tool improvements belong in both systems. Snell et al.
support budget/task dependence but exclude expensive difficulty estimation from
some reported comparisons; their figures do not justify a free router here [R4].

Power depends on discordant paired outcomes, not two independent accuracy SEs.
For independent pairs D in{-1,0,1}, Var(D)=q-delta^2. A normal planning calculation
at two-sided5%,80% power gives n≈7.85*(q-delta^2)/delta^2. At q=.06–.10:
  target2pp:1170–1955 independent pairs;
  target3pp:516–865;
  target5pp:181–307.
These are planning illustrations, not actual patient-cluster power. With an
illustrative variance inflation2,3pp requires1032–1730 pairs, so1397 is not a
promise of high power;2pp may remain poorly powered. Estimate/simulate uncertainty
using exposed patient-level paired outcomes and realistic new case mix, with
sensitivity to cluster/template dependence. Preserve the prespecified patient-
block bootstrap primary analysis. Do not count repeated calls on a question as
new independent patients/questions. Multiple frozen repetitions can estimate
stochastic variability, bundled within question/patient and averaged without
best-attempt selection, but need a NEW prospective development protocol.

Sparse-event reassurance is expensive: even under independent trials, zero harms
needs598 cases to put a one-sided95% upper rate below0.5%. No harms in a small
pilot therefore does not certify safe selection. Paired binary test research
also cautions that sparse discordances affect inference [R5].

6. Generalization and no peeking

The repeatedly adapted300 is a selection/development set; it cannot supply
independent validation. Cross-validation reshuffling after seeing its outcomes
does not restore that status [R6,R7]. The current1397 is question-disjoint but
mostly patient-overlapping (1379 questions on58 exposed patients); it tests new
question instances on largely familiar records. It cannot support new-patient,
untouched-template, new-task or cross-model claims. One18-question nominally
unexposed patient is not a powered external cohort. Patient-block intervals do
not remove adaptive selection or all template dependence.

Keep1397 wholly unused until one accepted source/runtime/scorer/cap freeze and
ALL8793 first attempts are terminal and audited. No partial accuracy or case
inspection; monitor operational metadata only. Keep lower95% bound>0 on1397 and
positive full2931 difference, all strata reported. A failed/inconclusive final
confirmation consumes that confirmation; further refinement needs new protected
data/protocol. A fresh-set decline is not automatically evidence of adaptive
overfitting; distribution shift is another explanation [R8].

Broader development can use the already excluded1174/60 pools; call those
broader development, not new validation. A later genuine patient-transfer claim
needs additional independently sourced patients and sufficiently many patient
clusters, with power planned before sampling. Synthea can provide fresh structural
stress tests but supports a synthetic-cohort claim. FHIRPath-QA on the same MIMIC
patients is new questions/tasks, not an independent patient cohort.

After a stable Luna method, freeze orchestration before testing other model
families against each one's strong Single, with explicit tool/schema/context
requirements. Generic model configuration is portability engineering, not
performance evidence. Preserve named operational caps while separately reporting
actual per-model costs; equal tokens across model families are not equal compute.
A frozen independent QA task/cohort is another axis of transfer. Retuning on its
outcomes spends that test as development; do not claim broad generalization from
one tuned model/task. Cross-model experiments are not requested at this stage.
