Cross-version failure analysis before V8 — 15 October 2026

Conclusion

No completed multi-agent design beat its contemporary capable Single. V3d
through V7 largely recovered a competent primary solver, but their auxiliary
mechanisms did not supply enough useful corrections. V7 is an engineering
milestone, not evidence of a scientific accuracy advance over V3. The next
candidate must demonstrate a mechanism before another large architecture run.

The strongest new empirical finding is persistent shared failure:37 of 300
questions are wrong in ALL 14 saved Single/Team arms across seven completed
versions.30 are temporal,5 lookup/counting and 2 numerical. The manual trace
review finds both correctable program behavior and ambiguous source/reference
conventions. It does not justify labeling all 37 as fixable reasoning errors.

Scope and evidence

The retrospective matrix re-scores all 4,200 saved first outcomes from original,
V2, completed usable V3d, V4, V5, V6 and V7, using the unchanged typed scorer.
All arms contain the same 300 IDs; errors remain incorrect. Source-file hashes,
cohort/scorer hashes, row-level flags and reproducible analysis are in empirical/.
Existing run-specific mechanism records remain authoritative for phase behavior.

This is adaptive development evidence from one backbone, not14 independent
models or a new validation set. Original/V2 do not retain every raw role
workspace, so later trace reconstruction is richer. Saved outputs contain no
SQL column; broader QA/SQL/reference files were not opened. No model/FHIR call,
candidate replay, protected-set evaluation or V8 implementation was performed.

Cross-version differences are not isolated topology effects. Shared prompts,

fresh stochastic controls also varied. In particular, original 162 to V7's 233
does not establish71 answers gained from agent collaboration. The contemporary

gains 12 and loses 26. These flips matter more than interpreting every new version
number as monotonic progress.

What each stage actually taught us

Original/V2: verification could destroy correct work. The original Team had 42
terminal failures versus Single's 16, and many wrong outcomes carried recorded
release issues. Earlier investigation found diagnostic objects replacing final
answers and overly large tool/history payloads. V2 improved the substrate, but
its 220 correct drafts lost 34 correct outcomes after verification and repaired12
wrong drafts. These transitions are not all verifier semantic judgments: release,
contract and scope failures also intervene. The combined system was still poor.

V3d combined integrated solving and removal of duplicate verification, yielding
a stronger observed primary. The promised repair mechanism was inactive. The
static audit ran on 283 source-valid primaries and produced no material local
repair. Solver source-feedback revisions
are a different mechanism; they must not be counted as static-audit successes.
The 6-answer gap to Single and zero targeted repairs do not support a useful
collaboration effect. The experiment established operation, not a winning critic.

V4: more planning was not automatically useful. Planning visibility, audit
removal and allocation changed together, so this is not a clean planning ablation.
Team had no terminal provider errors, while Single had one; service failures do
not explain the 21-answer deficit.25 of 29 paired losses were source-valid wrong
answers. Retained examples include incorrect time windows, ignoring deidentified
reference time, lost grouping, and changing correct computed content to satisfy
an output shape. A syntactically valid plan can misinterpret the task, and a
correct advisory plan can also be ignored by the solver.

V5: an inspect-and-patch interface was too weak in the observed run. The critic
could inspect only a frozen workspace, had two model replies and at most three
tools, and required an exact bounded patch. It released ZERO repairs. Of 64
eligible wrong primaries,49 ended KEEP/UNKNOWN and 15 encountered mechanical or
probe barriers. No rejected correct changing patch was identified. Source-related
withholding and solver source-feedback revisions remain separate: even restoring
all six computed-correct primaries later withheld would yield 231, below Single237.
Thus release fixes alone cannot explain away the deficit or establish critique.

V6: execution eligibility was not correction quality. B had no new acquisition
and a restrictive program/metadata contract. Many generated executions failed
contracts or runtime expectations; only 37 became eligible. But those 37 included
ZERO wrong-A/correct-B alternatives. Actual A scored 233; Team231 reflects one
harmful B selection and one correct-A auxiliary fatal loss. Mechanical coverage
and interfaces needed work, while selection alone had no corrective supply.

V7: much better candidate coverage still supplied almost no complementary answer.
Full-cap A scored 233. Across 268 actual B outcomes,174 were both correct,48 had
correct A/wrong B,45 were both wrong and ONE had wrong A/correct B. Eligibility
retained184; it lost no additional corrective released alternative to wrong A.
The one helpful selection was canceled by one auxiliary fatal loss of correct A.
Team233 therefore equals actual A 233; fresh Single scored 238. This does not make
the 5-answer Single/A difference an experimentally isolated effect of a policy:
initial saved messages matched, but they were distinct stochastic executions.

For those recorded candidates, even free perfect selection preserving A could
reach only 234 in V7, below the current minimum 240 selection gate; retaining the
observed auxiliary fatal loss lowers that to 233. This is a realized candidate
bound, not a theoretical ceiling on multi-agent systems. V7 also had 81 known B
budget stops among 268 entrants. Reported mean Team tokens were 1.59 times Single,
with unknown failed-call usage excluded. Equal maxima did not mean equal actual
resource use. Neither a selector rewrite nor a larger percentage of executable
alternatives addresses the observed shortage of useful alternatives.

Persistence and variation

All 7 Teams share44 wrong cases; all 5 recent Teams share46; all 7 Singles share43.
Only 109 cases are correct in all 14 arms; at least one arm succeeds on 263. These
are descriptive intersections of related, adaptively developed systems, not
population independence estimates or a deployable oracle ensemble. They show
both a shared difficult core and substantial answer turnover outside that core.
All 37 universal failures are nonerror wrong outcomes in V7;29 had no terminal
error in any of the 14 arms. Provider failures do not explain that persistent core.

Among the 37 universal failures, generic template metadata includes 9 specimen-
name questions,6 first/last medication-name questions,5 medication counts and 4
admission-type questions, plus smaller groups. These are offline diagnostic
strata. They must never become runtime template routes or answer mappings.

Retained-case analysis

An agent-assisted, purposive20-case review examined15 persistent all 14-wrong
cases plus 5 V4 regressions where V3d and V7 succeeded. Selection used observed
outcomes and task structure; review was not blinded. It is not a random sample
or a prevalence estimate. Root independently checked the actual programs for
the two medication examples, the specimen example and the global-max regression.
The 15 persistent A snapshots include 14 source-clean eligible releases and one
source-withheld result. The case packet distinguishes direct contradictions,
benchmark-projection ambiguity, source-reference uncertainty and common-gate
limitations, rather than giving every failure a confident semantic label.

Concrete program/requirement contradictions:

  Case383586: earliest medication during a visit. A sorts authoredOn, despite
  the shared prescription-start convention, and selects one row. Held records
  have 14 prescriptions tied at the earliest validity start, yielding9 distinct
  medication names exactly matching the reference set. This exposes both time-
  field and tied-set behavior using evidence already held by A.

  Case41b9a3: earliest IV prescription. A sorts validity starts and takes xs[0].
  Three held requests tie at the minimum, with 3 distinct names exactly matching
  the reference set. Citations support the returned member but cannot establish
  complete-set correctness. No new retrieval is necessary to expose the omission.

  Case65f468, V4: question explicitly requests monthly maxima. The actual program
  computes one global max and returns a scalar. A source check can validate that
  scalar's input fields while missing the lost grouping requirement.

  Other reviewed V4 regressions:00b98d drops the month and uses the current rather
  than previous year;461ed0 andd6121 apply a host-year2026 cutoff to deidentified
  future dates;f517e3 changes a computed monthly-average dictionary to month-label
  strings after contract rejection, while still claiming to return averages.
  These cases demonstrate failure modes, not proof that the planner caused them.

Projection ambiguity, not a clean natural-language oracle:

  Case7866a9 says 'name of the specimen test'. A returns the Observation's test
  name; the reference expects specimen type. The selected Observation links to
  a held Specimen whose type is exactly the reference. This demonstrates an
  available alternative projection, but the wording admits confusion; a generic
  semantic verifier is not guaranteed to infer the benchmark's intended one.
  Case2d7404 similarly returns a test label; the intended source cohort/projection
  needs care. Do not add a rule that every specimen question means one source.

Source/convention questions that cannot be called obvious agent repairs:

  Admission-type cases1f2e37 anddbadc6 return admitSource. Held encounter classes
  carry a different related concept; exact legacy reference labels are absent.
  Establishing a faithful terminology mapping needs adapter/data review, not
  answer-specific synonym injection or an LLM pretending to know the mapping.

  Casec04f16 asks first microbiology time. A/B choose an earlier held May test;
  the reference is a later December test, also held, with no visible date filter
  explaining that restriction. Hidden source/translation intent is unresolved.

  Casef8d725 pools equal-display PTT observations from laboratory/chart systems.
  The minimum of that pool precedes the reference; a chart-only cohort would
  select the reference. The visible question does not state that cohort. This
  is a source-selection ambiguity, not established timezone arithmetic failure.

  Casebf8aee's reference time was not found in held request fields checked;
  case2ca14c has 3 matching prescriptions without numeric doses, while380 is the
  sum of known doses and the output carries an honest limitation. Missing source
  values do not authorize inventing a dose or assuming the reference is wrong.

Common source-gate limitation:

  Case967175 has organism records with encounter information reachable through
  held parent observations. The released result is withheld for missing direct
  encounter links. This motivates a shared adapter/source-validator review with
  independently specified graph fixtures. A fix would belong in Single too;
  it would not establish a benefit from agent collaboration. The next proposed
  investigator initially skips withheld A artifacts and will not solve this case.

The review's 7 direct contradictions comprise2 persistent medication cases and
5 selected V4 regressions. The 2 specimen cases form a separate ambiguous class.
Other labels remain narrower or unresolved. Neither these counts nor14 clean
source checks imply a measured distribution of all 300 root causes. Exact case
IDs, sources, retained observations and uncertainty are in empirical/case_review.json.

Implications for the next design and process

1. Test a distinct information-producing task. A verifier should probe the
   actual program's question obligations and retain concrete counterexamples;
   another whole solve or another static critique is weakly motivated here.

2. Test derived evidence before assuming missing retrieval is dominant. Strong
   examples already have the needed records. Separate workspace-only versus
   retrieval-enabled investigation instead of bundling both with a new planner.

3. Separate shared adapter/data repairs from coordination. Timestamps, graph
   ownership, event identity and reference ambiguities need common rules and a
   refreshed Single control. Keep official scores unchanged while independently
   documenting potential reference problems. Do not filter difficult cases out.

4. Require mechanism evidence before another 300. Count wrong-A/correct-B supply,
   helpful/harmful switches and fatal losses over all scheduled cases. A clearer
   trace and passing offline tests are necessary engineering, not efficacy.

5. Keep architecture claims smaller than the evidence. Plan visibility was
   bundled; Single/A differences include stochasticity; correct null credit is
   not proof of a completed absence search. Causal ablations and protected new
   questions must precede claims of coordination or generalization.

The proposed study, advancement/rejection rules, budget feasibility work and
generalization plan are in proposed_design_and_tests.txt. It proposes one bounded
investigator hypothesis and controlled tests. It does not authorize restarting
the paused goal, implement V8 or claim that the next system will win.
