Implication for a hypothesis, not another selected design

The supported failure target is semantic query execution and evidence sufficiency,
not simply 'more independent answers'. V7 already supplied fresh retrieval and
natural solving; its observed268 B outcomes contained one corrective alternative.
Blind rerolls cannot resolve a shared wrong field/cohort convention or a missing
source quantity. A next falsifiable hypothesis is that an independently stated
query obligation plus targeted counterevidence can expose a wrong operation or
missing evidence more often than another whole solve, at equal charged budget.
Examples of gold-free obligations are output entity/type, allowed time anchor,
event source/cohort, extrema tie policy, group-then-aggregate order and explicit
provenance edges. These are generic task semantics, not routes keyed to the20 IDs.

Success criteria must include wrong-A counterexamples actually found, correct-A
damage, evidence acquisition success, accepted repairs and end-to-end gain.
Generating a plausible query spec, passing citations or changing an answer is
not success. Keep unsupported source/cohort ambiguity as UNKNOWN and preserve
the capable primary. Any reservation that changes its solving budget is a new
policy bundle requiring a fresh fair control; cross-version score changes do
not isolate it. Before spending on V8, audit exposed-case SQL/FHIR mapping where
source/intent remains ambiguous, without changing official scoring posthoc.

Beyond300 and limits

Use the existing reserved development/pilot patients and specification-generated
fixtures to vary ties, temporal boundaries, labels/systems, empty/incomplete input,
relation chains, units and grouped output. Do not add these exact answers or
retrospective categories to runtime. Judge failures on unseen synthetic variants,
not fixtures that only restate a repaired example. Broader reserved-patient tests
can expose coverage failures without consuming the protected question stratum.

The existing prospective protocol remains the constraint: after development
selection, one frozen2931 three-arm run; no partial confirmation scoring; all
first-attempt failures retained; integrity before analysis; primary Team−Single
on1397 question-disjoint IDs,95% question-weighted patient bootstrap10000/5106,
lower bound>0 plus positive full2931 difference. Report every prespecified stratum.
It is not patient-disjoint:1379/1397 belong to previously exposed patients; the
one nominally unexposed patient has18 questions and cannot justify population
patient transfer. A failed confirmation cannot be retuned and called held-out.
Cross-model and independent-task transfer require separate future controls/data;
current results establish neither. Nothing here spends or inspects confirmation.

Reproduction/provenance

cross_version.py streams14 actual saved arrays, asserts exact ordered fixed300,
question/reference/patient equality and published totals, then produces the
matrix and intersections with the unchanged pure scorer. It does not call the
full-CSV compare entrypoint. extract_cases.py andextract_version_specific.py read

inspection; no saved agent program is executed. No model/FHIR/network/config,
Git/source/record mutation or deleted experiment recreation occurred.
