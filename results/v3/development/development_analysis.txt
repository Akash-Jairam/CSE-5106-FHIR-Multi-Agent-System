HISTORICAL COLLECTION AND SCORING METHOD
The development experiment scored every scheduled first attempt under its original protocol. Its original outcomes have been preserved. The later full benchmark selects the first runtime-clean outcome under a different collection policy. Keep those two estimands separate rather than retroactively rewriting development results or pooling the observations.
Team per-question correctness and mechanism scores were reused from frozen reports. Fresh Single Agent correctness was recomputed offline with the unchanged frozen typed scorer, then reconciled exactly to 238/300 and every frozen family count. No inference or provider request was made.

SOURCE FEEDBACK, AUDIT AND PHASES
The team produced 283 source-valid primary releases. Source feedback triggered 19 revisions, with four wrong-to-right draft/primary transitions and no losses at that transition. The static audit inspected 283 valid primaries, proposed no material local repair, and triggered zero targeted revisions or wrong-primary recoveries.
Five initially correct drafts were not credited at final release; this is a descriptive transition, not evidence that the audit caused those losses. Six null credits lacked a source-valid release. Preserve that distinction when discussing answer accuracy versus evidential support.
Observed phase records: contract 300, solver 300, audit 283, targeted 0. Recorded phase token sums: contract 432,057, solver 13,817,221, audit 1,258,421. The solver executed 1,545 tools. Per-question phase caps and numeric phase accounting appear in phase_measurements.csv; all frozen mechanism definitions remain available in the original diagnostics.
